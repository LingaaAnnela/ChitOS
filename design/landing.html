Features

Built for every circle.

Everything an organiser needs — without the complexity of enterprise chit fund software.

01

Multi-group console

Manage friends, family, and office circles from one dashboard. Each with its own rules, members, and cycle history.

02

Four payout strategies

Auction, lottery, fixed order, or consensus. You pick the strategy that fits your group — change it each cycle.

03

Smart interest engine

Early takers pay a fair premium. Patient waiters earn a reward. Every calculation shown transparently to all members.

04

Member approval flow

Every member who clicks your invite link needs your approval before joining. No strangers, no surprises.

05

Mid-cycle import

Already running a chit on paper or WhatsApp? Import your history and track going forward from any point.

06

Trust scores

A 0–100 score per member based on payment consistency, auction participation, and tenure. Know who to trust.

How it works

Up and running in minutes.

Step 01

Create your group

Set contribution amount, pay frequency, max members, and payout strategy in under 2 minutes.

Step 02

Invite via WhatsApp

Share a link. Members request to join and you approve each one. Group chat is already in your pocket.

Step 03

Run the cycle

Track contributions, open the auction room, declare the winner, and log the payout from your phone.

Step 04

Close and repeat

Export a one-page PDF summary, view the full ledger, and start the next cycle with one tap.
